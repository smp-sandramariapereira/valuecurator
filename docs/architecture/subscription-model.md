# Evidence plans

`basic` admits `pyth-hermes` and does not allow fallback.

`operator` admits `pyth-pro` and `pyth-hermes`, and may allow fallback.

The server plan comes from `KAIROS_SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN`. A plan id in the browser body cannot widen that plan. `KAIROS_ENTITLEMENTS_TOKEN` is the bearer secret for the entitlements route. The route does not echo it.

These plans do not set the illustrative 1 USDC Devnet transfer and do not buy the asset. They do not enter the Devnet program.
